# Review Board Deliberation and Final Rankings

The board has concluded its technical review of the 9 Android devices.

### SECTION 1: THE DELIBERATION TRANSCRIPT

**Round 1: Camera and Hardware Clash**
Marcus: The camera directive dictates our primary focus. The iQOO 12 and Xiaomi 14 offer the best raw imaging hardware. The iQOO 12 uses an OV50H main sensor and OV64B periscope.
Priya: The iQOO 12 relies on a virtual proximity sensor. The front video microphone also records inconsistent audio quality.
Marcus: The camera hardware offsets those drawbacks. GCam users specifically target the OV64B sensor for telephoto photography. The Xiaomi 14 provides a triple 50MP sensor array.
Alex: The Xiaomi 14 costs ₹59,999. The OnePlus 13 costs ₹69,999 and uses the LYT-808 sensor.
Priya: The OnePlus 13 has serious microphone issues. Users report muffled audio during calls. The high price makes this device uncompetitive.
Marcus: I agree. We eliminate the OnePlus 13 from top contention. The Nothing Phone (4a) Pro includes a 50MP periscope lens. This hardware makes it a strong camera contender at ₹43,979.

**Round 2: Background Service Stress Test**
Alex: We must evaluate background process survival. The user pays for BuzzKill and runs Syncthing continuously. BBK and Xiaomi operating systems aggressively kill these applications.
Priya: The iQOO 12, Xiaomi 14, and OnePlus 13R all fail the non-root Tasker test.
Alex: Exactly. NothingOS passes the Tasker survival test natively. The Nothing Phone (4a) Pro keeps Anki and Obsidian running in the background without manual intervention.
Marcus: Camera hardware remains the absolute primary criterion. The iQOO 12 and Xiaomi 14 capture better optical data. The user must manually disable battery optimizations on those devices.
Alex: Manual overrides do not guarantee survival on FuntouchOS or HyperOS. The Nothing Phone (4a) Pro provides the best software balance.
Marcus: The Xiaomi 14 Civi and OnePlus 13R offer good camera hardware for lower prices. The Oppo Reno16 costs ₹61,999 but uses a small LYT-600 sensor. We drop the Reno16 entirely.

**Round 3: Final Consensus**
Marcus: The iQOO 12 takes first place. The OV50H and OV64B combination provides excellent raw data for GCam.
Alex: I accept this ranking because camera hardware is the primary directive. The Xiaomi 14 takes second place for its triple 50MP array and physical proximity sensor.
Priya: The Nothing Phone (4a) Pro secures third place. It passes the background survival test. It also provides reliable microphones and a 50MP periscope lens.
Marcus: The Xiaomi 14 Civi claims fourth place. It features an OVX8000 sensor and physical proximity sensor for ₹44,999.
Alex: The OnePlus 13R takes fifth place. It offers a Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 and LYT-700 sensor at an aggressive ₹35,999 price point.

### SECTION 3: TOP 5 DEEP DIVES

**1. iQOO 12**
(a) The iQOO 12 features an OmniVision OV50H primary sensor with optical image stabilization. The secondary 64MP OV64B periscope telephoto lens provides strong optical reach. This specific sensor combination offers high compatibility with GCam and MotionCam. The camera hardware exposes raw sensor streams effectively.
(b) FuntouchOS applies strict battery optimization protocols. The operating system fails the non-root Tasker survival test. Users must manually disable battery restrictions for BuzzKill and Syncthing. These background applications may still face unexpected termination during heavy memory loads.
(c) The device uses a virtual proximity sensor for screen state management. The earpiece outputs very loud and crisp audio. The main microphone captures clear voice data during cellular calls. Front-facing video microphone performance remains inconsistent across different apps.

**2. Xiaomi 14**
(a) The Xiaomi 14 integrates a 50MP primary sensor with a fast f/1.6 aperture. The 75mm telephoto and 115-degree ultrawide lenses complete the triple 50MP array. This hardware provides complete auxiliary lens access for third-party camera applications. The setup supports high-bitrate video capture through MotionCam.
(b) HyperOS implements strict background process limits by default. The system kills idle applications to preserve battery life. Syncthing and Anki require manual battery exception configurations. The user interface remains highly fluid during daily navigation tasks.
(c) The phone includes a physical proximity sensor for accurate screen blanking. The hybrid stereo setup uses the earpiece as a top speaker. This configuration delivers loud and balanced audio output. The microphone array captures high-quality sound without distortion.

**3. Nothing Phone (4a) Pro**
(a) This phone uses the Sony LYT-700C primary sensor with optical stabilization. A 50MP periscope telephoto lens provides long-range optical zoom capabilities. The 8MP ultrawide lens captures wider scenes but lacks high resolution. GCam auxiliary access functions properly across all three rear lenses.
(b) NothingOS excels at background service management. The device passes the non-root Tasker survival test completely. BuzzKill, Syncthing, and Obsidian sync run continuously without system interference. The Snapdragon 7 Gen 4 processor ensures smooth user interface navigation.
(c) The manufacturer uses a virtual proximity sensor. The device features clear stereo speakers that output loud audio. The standard microphone setup maintains consistent voice clarity. The hardware provides reliable daily usability without major audio compromises.

**4. Xiaomi 14 Civi**
(a) The primary camera uses a 50MP OmniVision OVX8000 sensor with optical stabilization. A 50MP Samsung JN1 telephoto lens complements the main camera. The 12MP ultrawide lens provides basic wide-angle photography capabilities. This hardware combination supports direct raw image extraction for GCam.
(b) The device runs on a Snapdragon 8s Gen 3 processor. The MIUI operating system aggressively terminates background processes. The phone fails the Tasker survival test. Users must manually lock critical apps like BuzzKill in memory.
(c) A physical proximity sensor ensures reliable screen control during calls. The stereo speakers produce loud audio for voice communications. The speaker output lacks low-end bass frequencies and fine detail. The microphone transmits crisp audio during standard phone calls.

**5. OnePlus 13R**
(a) The OnePlus 13R equips a 50MP Sony LYT-700 primary sensor. A dedicated 50MP telephoto lens provides high-resolution zoomed images. An 8MP ultrawide sensor handles wide-angle photography. The camera software allows full auxiliary lens access for GCam modifications.
(b) The Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 chip delivers extremely fast system performance. OxygenOS applies aggressive battery management rules to background applications. The device fails the Tasker survival evaluation. Continuous background synchronization for Obsidian requires manual battery optimization overrides.
(c) The device uses a virtual proximity sensor system. The stereo earpiece outputs exceptionally loud audio. The tonal balance differs slightly from the primary bottom speaker. The microphones provide standard voice clarity for daily phone calls.
